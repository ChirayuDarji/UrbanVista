Help Center

Find answers to common questions and learn how to use UrbanVista

Getting Started

How to Submit a Report

  1. Click on "Report Issue" button in the navigation or dashboard
  2. Select the type of issue (Road, Water, Garbage, etc.)
  3. Enter a detailed description of the problem
  4. Provide the exact location where the issue is located
  5. Upload a photo if available (optional but recommended)
  6. Click "Submit Report" to send it to the authorities

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my report status?
You can track your report status by visiting "My Reports" in your dashboard. Each report shows its current status (Pending, In Progress, Resolved, etc.) and you'll receive email notifications when the status changes.
How long does it take to resolve an issue?
Resolution time varies depending on the type and complexity of the issue. Simple issues like garbage collection may be resolved within 24-48 hours, while infrastructure issues like road repairs may take longer. You can check the average resolution time in the Statistics page.
Can I edit or delete my report?
You can only edit or delete reports that are still in "Pending" status. Once a report is assigned or in progress, you cannot modify it. However, you can always add comments or additional information through attachments.
What types of issues can I report?
You can report various civic issues including: Road problems (potholes, damaged roads), Water leakage, Garbage collection issues, Broken streetlights, Sewage and drainage problems, and Other civic issues. Select "Other" if your issue doesn't fit the listed categories.
Is my personal information safe?
Yes, we take your privacy seriously. Your personal information is only shared with relevant municipal authorities for issue resolution purposes. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Read our Privacy Policy for more details.
How do I contact support?
You can contact our support team through the Contact page. Fill out the contact form with your query and we'll get back to you within 24-48 hours. For urgent issues, you can also reach out to the relevant municipal department directly.

Guides

Adding Location to Reports

When submitting a report, be as specific as possible about the location:

  • Include the street name and area
  • Mention nearby landmarks if available
  • If you have GPS coordinates, the system will automatically capture them
  • You can also click on the map to set the exact location

Taking Good Photos

  • Take clear, well-lit photos of the issue
  • Include multiple angles if possible
  • Make sure the photo shows the problem clearly
  • Include surrounding context to help authorities locate the issue

Managing Notifications

You'll receive email notifications for:

  • When your report is submitted successfully
  • When the status of your report changes
  • When your report is resolved
  • Important updates from authorities

You can manage notification preferences in your account settings.
